The website doptiva.com does not use cookies for advertising, profiling or tracking visitors. That is why we do not show a consent banner.

## What is stored in your browser

| Name | Purpose | Duration | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| doptiva-theme | remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | until you delete it | browser local storage (localStorage) |
| doptiva-newsletter-dismissed | remembers that you dismissed the newsletter signup for the current session | until the session ends | browser session storage (sessionStorage) |

This value is stored only if you switch the theme yourself. It stays in your browser, is never sent to us and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings. Because this storage is explicitly requested by you with a click, it does not require consent under the Slovenian Electronic Communications Act (ZEKom-2).

## Visit statistics

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Data is shown only in aggregate form.

## External content

We host our fonts on our own server, so your IP address is not sent to Google or other font providers when you visit. The site has no embedded videos, maps, ads or social media buttons.

## Changes

If we ever introduce cookies that require consent, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any such cookie is stored.
