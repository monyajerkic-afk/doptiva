This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit doptiva.com or send us an enquiry, why we process it and what rights you have.

## What data we process

**Enquiries via the form or email.** We process the data you enter yourself: your name, email address, optionally your company and phone number, and the content of your message.

**Visiting the site.** Our hosting provider automatically records technical data on each visit, such as IP address, time of visit, requested page and browser type. This data is needed for the site to run securely and reliably.

<!--analytics-->
**Visit statistics.** We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not track individuals across websites and shows data only in aggregate form (for example, the number of page views).
<!--/analytics-->

## Purpose and legal basis

- **Replying to your enquiry and preparing a proposal or quote**: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).
- **Security and operation of the site**: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
<!--analytics-->
- **Visit statistics**: our legitimate interest in understanding which content is useful to visitors (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
<!--/analytics-->

We do not sell data, use it for advertising or make automated decisions with it.

## How long we keep data

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months after the last exchange of messages if no collaboration follows. If you become a client, we keep data for as long as needed to perform the contract and then for as long as the law requires, for example tax law. The hosting provider keeps technical visit data only briefly, in line with its own rules.

## Email newsletter

You can voluntarily subscribe with your email address and separate, explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). We send a confirmation email and add your address to our newsletter list only after you click the confirmation link (double opt-in). We use this list for occasional practical advice and Doptiva updates. We keep your email until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. You can unsubscribe using the link in each marketing email or by writing to {{email}}. Resend delivers newsletters and manages subscriptions, processing data in the US under appropriate contractual safeguards. Sending an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you.

## Your rights

You may at any time request access to your data, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send your request to {{email}}. We reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, [www.ip-rs.si](https://www.ip-rs.si/en/).

## Cookies

This site does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. Details are in the [cookie policy](/en/legal/cookies/).
