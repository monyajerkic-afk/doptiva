What we do

Four areas that complement each other. We can start with any of them, depending on what is holding you back today.

Consulting

Understanding business needs, reviewing existing processes and choosing improvements that make sense.

  • Review of processes, tools and data
  • Prioritised list of improvements with expected impact
  • Independent advice when choosing software

Digitalisation and automation

Simplifying repetitive work and helping you introduce the right tools.

  • Digital forms instead of paper and retyping
  • Connected tools, so data is never entered twice
  • Automatic reports, notifications and approvals

Digital solutions

Design, prototyping and development of solutions within our expertise or with partners.

  • Design and prototype before you commit to development
  • Smaller web applications and internal tools
  • Larger builds delivered with trusted partners

Training and support

Practical help for employees and business owners using digital tools and AI.

  • Workshops built on your own cases
  • Rules for safe use of AI in your company
  • Support after roll-out, when questions come up

We choose solutions based on your needs, not on tools we would like to sell. Where larger development is needed, we bring in external specialists and tell you so up front.

How working together goes

No long kick-off meetings and no obligation. The first step is a short message.

  1. You describe what is holding you back

    A few sentences in the form are enough. No technical terms needed.

  2. We review and ask

    If something is unclear, we ask. We reply within two working days.

  3. We propose a first concrete step

    You get a proposal with a clear scope, timeline and price before anything starts.

  4. We deliver and stay available

    We introduce the solution with you and help until you use it on your own.

Understanding first. Technology second.

You do not need to know what LLM, API or workflow mean to understand what we can do for you. That is our job.

Your problem comes first: the time you lose, the mistakes that repeat and the data you cannot find. Technology comes in only once it is clear what it has to solve.

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, write to us. We reply within two working days.

Who is Doptiva for?

Small and medium-sized businesses and entrepreneurs who lose too much time to manual work, retyping and searching for data. We know hospitality, tourism, field sales and multi-location businesses particularly well.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope. After a first review you receive a written proposal with a clear scope and price, so you know what you are deciding on. Our reply to your enquiry and the proposal for a first step are free of obligation.

Do we need to understand AI or programming?

No. You know your work, we know the tools. We talk in the language of your business and handle the technical details ourselves.

Can we use the tools we already have?

Yes, and that is often the best place to start. A lot can be improved in Excel, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 before new tools make sense.

Do you build custom software?

We build smaller solutions, prototypes and internal tools ourselves. For larger projects we work with trusted external specialists, which we state in the proposal.

Where do you work and in which languages?

We are based in Ribnica, Slovenia, and work with companies across Slovenia remotely. We work in Slovenian and English.

How do you handle data protection?

We handle your data in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Where possible we choose tools that store data in the EU, and we tell you in advance which providers will have access to it.

Tell us what is holding you back.

We reply within two working days with a proposal for a first concrete step. No obligation.
